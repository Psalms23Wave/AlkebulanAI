## Becoming a maintainer

We look for contributors who have:

- Landed multiple substantive pull requests over at least 3 months.
- Demonstrated good judgement in reviews and issue triage.
- Shown a commitment to the project's mission and community.

Maintainers are appointed by the BDFL. If you are interested, reach
out through
[psalms23wave.com/contact](https://www.psalms23wave.com/contact)
(subject: *"Maintainer application"*) with links to your
contributions and a short note on how you would like to help.
