# Commercial & Enterprise Licensing

The Alkebulan AI code (ABLT tokenizer + Sekan algorithm) is released
under the **Apache License 2.0**. You can already use it in commercial
products without asking — that is the whole point of Apache 2.0.

This page exists for the cases where that **default is not enough**
for your use case.

---

## When you might need a commercial license from us

You should contact **Psalms23Wave LLC** if you need any of the
following:

- **Proprietary re-licensing.** Your product ships under a licence
  incompatible with Apache 2.0, and you need to remove or replace
  the NOTICE file / attribution requirements.
- **Trademark usage rights.** You want to name your product, service,
  fork, or company something that uses "Alkebulan AI", "ABLT", or
  "Sekan" — see [`TRADEMARK.md`](./TRADEMARK.md).
- **Private / custom language packs.** You want us to build or
  co-develop a morphology rule layer for a specific African
  language (Swahili, Yoruba, Amharic, etc.) tailored to your
  corpus or domain.
- **Enterprise support & SLA.** You want paid support, guaranteed
  response times, security advisories, or on-call consulting.
- **Custom fine-tuning services.** You want us to fine-tune a
  base model (Mistral, Llama, etc.) on your proprietary corpus
  using ABLT + Sekan, and deliver weights + inference code.
- **Warranty & indemnification.** Your procurement process
  requires a warranted license (Apache 2.0 is provided "AS IS").
- **Dual licensing for distributors.** You want to redistribute
  modified versions under your own terms.
- **Training / workshops.** You want private training on ABLT +
  Sekan for your engineering team or research group.

## How to get in touch

The fastest, cleanest way is the **Psalms23Wave contact form**:

> **[psalms23wave.com/contact](https://www.psalms23wave.com/contact)** — *Send Us a Message*

It takes a few seconds to fill out (First Name, Last Name, Email,
Subject, Message) and routes the enquiry straight to us. Please use:

- **Subject:** `Commercial licensing — Alkebulan AI`
- **Message** — please include:
  1. Your organisation (legal name and country).
  2. A short description of what you want to do (use case, product,
     geography, expected scale).
  3. Which specific component(s) you need the commercial terms for:
     code, model weights, trademarks, services, or all of the above.
  4. A timeline, if there is one.

We will reply with a scoping call, a proposed license structure,
and indicative pricing. Response time is normally within **5 business
days**.

## Our philosophy

We release this project open-source because we genuinely believe the
African AI industry needs a shared foundation to build on. At the
same time, the Company needs a way to fund continued research —
building rule layers for new languages, training larger base models
on African corpora, and keeping the infrastructure alive.

Commercial licensing is how we do that sustainably. If you want to
use this work in a serious commercial setting, please support the
research that produced it by licensing it from us. The proceeds go
directly into extending the project to the next languages on the
roadmap.
