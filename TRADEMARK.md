## Why this matters

The Marks represent the reputation, research, and integrity of a
specific body of work. Allowing anyone to ship a modified, unsafe, or
low-quality fork under the same name would:

1. Confuse users about what they are actually running.
2. Damage the reputation of the research.
3. Undermine the trust that makes the open-source contribution model
   work.

Trademark protection and open-source licensing are **complementary,
not contradictory**. Projects like Linux® (Linus Torvalds / Linux
Foundation), Kubernetes® (CNCF), Python® (PSF), and Apache® (ASF) all
follow this model: open code + reserved name.
